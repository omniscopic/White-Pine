The Idea

Everyone needs a home. This project is a celebration of home through love of local trees, wood, water and an appreciation of creative craft.

Dr. Rich Blundell proposes a wooden surfboard residency at the Art Complex Museum. Locally sourced New England white pine becomes hollow wooden surfboards, built in the open. Young people learn creativity and craftsmanship in a community build. Music from the region's emerging artists carries the stoke.

It is a homecoming, and a return with the elixir: a celebration of the beauty of wood, the places we come from, and positive vibes for the future.

The Builder

The Provenance of Rich

Rich is an ecologist, natural historian, and scientist-in-residence at the Maria Mitchell Association on Nantucket. Duxbury, Massachusetts is his home habitat, and he is a Cape Cod and Islands organism.

He has been building hollow wooden surfboards for twenty years. His designs and build-methods are used all over the world. No factory, no molds, no shortcuts. Just a design, a stack of wood, and a plane.
